how sessions work with spotify

pick a mood and start a session. moodtunes finds new songs from the genres of your songs in that mood, and anything you play in spotify while the timer runs is added to the session.

spotify premium
  1. start spotify playing on any device (phone, laptop, speaker)
  2. start a session here. every recommended song is added to your spotify queue
  3. keep listening in spotify. ▶ on a song here plays it straight away
  4. liked the mix? tap also save as playlist to keep it in your spotify

spotify isn't open yet? play anything there and the songs are added within a few seconds.

spotify free
  1. start a session here to get your recommendations
  2. tap save as spotify playlist and it opens in spotify
  3. press shuffle play there and listen as normal

spotify doesn't let free accounts add songs to the queue from other apps, so a playlist is the way to listen to a whole session.
